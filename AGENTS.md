- Daily and weekly guardian reports share one page and date-range helper; WhatsApp deep links track conversation opening, never delivery, because only provider evidence can confirm sending.

- Memorization summaries must materialize each student's evidence once per call before computing complete/partial hizbs; repeated parsing for every hizb causes circle eligibility timeouts.
- Himmah day circles and participants save through one atomic RPC (himmah_save_plan); unapproved attempts are cancelled, never deleted; certificate images load via himmah_branding outside the polled dashboard. Why: partial saves and silent removals lost data, and inline images made every 8-second sync heavy.
- Himmah hizb plans are free lists ("1-5، 8"), new students start unselected, and pause time is capped per day by pause_limit_seconds (0 = unlimited) with the same rule mirrored in himmah_elapsed and elapsedSeconds. Why: contiguous ranges and pre-selected students enrolled wrong hizbs, and an unbounded pause bypassed the 540-second limit.
